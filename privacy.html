Privacy policy

DAKboard Daily Photos is a personal Google Apps Script with a single user, its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

The script runs inside the owner's own Google account. It uses Google account access only to:

  • create and manage the Google Photos albums and images that the script itself uploads;
  • read image files from one Google Drive folder that the owner chose;
  • download public images from the web and send them to an image-resizing service.

The script collects no personal information, stores no data outside the owner's Google account beyond the temporary image copies used for resizing, and shares nothing with third parties. It has no analytics, advertising, or tracking.
